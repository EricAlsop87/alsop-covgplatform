# California FAIR Plan (CFP) & DIC Companion Policy Rules

This skill governs the handling of California FAIR Plan (CFP) fire policies and Difference in Conditions (DIC) companion coverage.

---

### California FAIR Plan (CFP) Context

1. **Fire-Only Scope**:
   - The California FAIR Plan provides coverage primarily for direct physical loss caused by Fire and Lightning (and optional Extended Coverage).
   - It does NOT cover Personal Liability, Water Damage, Theft, or Medical Payments.

2. **Companion DIC (Difference in Conditions) Recommendation**:
   - When drafting emails for clients with a standalone CFP policy, gently remind them of the importance of maintaining a companion DIC policy to cover essential non-fire perils (Liability, Water Damage, Theft, Vandalism).
   - Phrasing: *"Because your California FAIR Plan policy focuses on fire protection, having a companion DIC policy ensures you are covered for liability, water damage, and personal property."*

3. **360Value Replacement Cost Estimate (RCE)**:
   - When an updated RCE link is available, reference it as an updated estimate of today's local reconstruction costs.
   - Phrasing: *"We've included an updated Replacement Cost Estimate reflecting current local building costs for your property."*
