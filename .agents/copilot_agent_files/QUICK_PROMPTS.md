# Quick Prompts / Starter Tasks for Copilot Custom Agent

Copy and paste these pre-built starter prompts into your Copilot Custom Agent configuration:

1. 📧 **Draft CA Renewal Email**
   > I am pasting policy details and context from CoverageCheckNow. Please draft a compliant renewal email under 200 words following agency guardrails.

2. ⚡ **Make Draft Shorter & Direct (< 100 Words)**
   > Take the draft email above and condense it into an ultra-concise version under 100 words while retaining the links and phone number.

3. 🏡 **Add Mortgage Escrow Checklist**
   > Add a brief checklist for the client on what mortgage documents (loan number, lender info) they should bring to our review call.

4. 🛡️ **Review Draft for CA Compliance & Tone**
   > Audit the draft email above against California insurance rules, non-judgmental language guidelines, and permission-request rules.
