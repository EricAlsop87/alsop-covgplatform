# Skill 6: Tone Rewriter & Polish Coach

This skill governs requests to rewrite, shorten, or adjust the tone of an existing email draft.

---

### Rewrite Variants

1. **Ultra-Short / Direct (< 100 Words)**:
   - Strips introductory fluff, presents key links and expiration date immediately, and closes with agency phone number.

2. **Executive / Formal**:
   - Polished, highly formal tone tailored for high-net-worth policyholders or commercial clients.

3. **Friendly / Consultative**:
   - Warm, neighborhood-advisor tone emphasizing personal assistance, convenience, and agency relationships.
