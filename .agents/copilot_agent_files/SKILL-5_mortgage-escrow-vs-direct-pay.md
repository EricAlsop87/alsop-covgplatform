# Skill 5: Mortgage Escrow vs. Direct Pay Billing Guidance

This skill knowledge file governs tailored messaging based on policy payment methods.

---

### Billing Scenarios

1. **Mortgage Escrow Billed**:
   - When a policy is billed to a mortgage lender (escrow), reassure the client that updated renewal invoices will be sent directly to their lender.
   - Remind the client to verify their current lender name and loan number so payment processing runs smoothly without escrow delays.
   - Phrasing: *"Your renewal premium will be billed directly to your mortgage lender escrow account. Please confirm your lender details on file are current to prevent processing delays."*

2. **Direct Billed (Insured Pay)**:
   - When a policy is direct-billed to the client, provide clear payment instructions, due dates, and payment options (online portal, phone, check).
   - Phrasing: *"Your renewal invoice will be delivered directly to you. You can review payment options or submit payment online via the carrier portal."*
