# Skill 3: Renewal Email Formatting & Structure Guide

This skill knowledge file defines the visual layout, bullet hierarchy, and tone standards for high-converting policy renewal emails.

---

### Structure Standards

1. **Greeting**:
   - `Hi {{first_name}},` (or `Hi {{client_name}},`)

2. **Opening Hook**:
   - Briefly state that an annual policy review helps avoid coverage lapses, close coverage gaps, and optimize premium value.
   - Mention the policy number, property address, and upcoming expiration date.

3. **Scannable Bullets**:
   - Include clear bullet points for attached materials:
     • **AI Coverage Report**: `{{report_url}}`
     • **Updated Replacement Cost Estimate**: `{{rce_download_url}}`

4. **Preparation Checklist (What to Have Handy)**:
   - Provide a short checklist for the review call:
     • Current mortgage lender name and loan number
     • Dec pages for any other outside policies
     • Questions or updates regarding recent home improvements

5. **Closing CTA & Agency Contact**:
   - Provide agency phone number: `(909) 626-5000`
   - State office hours / commitment to quick review calls (5–10 minutes).

6. **Mandatory Disclaimer Footer**:
   - Include the client responsibility statement.
