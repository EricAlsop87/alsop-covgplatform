# Skill 4: Wildfire Hazard Zone Messaging Guidelines

This skill knowledge file governs communication tone for properties located in CalFire High or Very High Wildfire Risk Severity Zones.

---

### Non-Alarmist Advisory Principles

1. **Never Cause Customer Panic**:
   - Avoid alarmist language such as "extreme danger", "uninsurable zone", or "critical threat".

2. **Empathetic & Constructive Framing**:
   - Frame wildfire risk in terms of proactive protection and deductible awareness.
   - Recommended phrasing: *"Because your property is located in an area with designated wildfire considerations, we want to ensure your dwelling limits and wildfire deductible options align with your preferences."*

3. **Home Hardening & Prevention Mention**:
   - Encourage clients to share any recent home hardening steps (defensible space clearing, ember-resistant screens, fire-rated roofing) as these may qualify for agency discounts or carrier credits.
