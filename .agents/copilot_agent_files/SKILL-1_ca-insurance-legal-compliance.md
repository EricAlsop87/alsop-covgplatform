# Skill 1: California Insurance Legal Compliance & Guardrails

This skill knowledge file governs legal compliance, disclaimers, and regulatory boundaries under the California Insurance Code (CIC) for Alsop & Associates Insurance Agency.

---

### Key Compliance Rules

1. **Non-Binding Notice**:
   - Email drafts must state or imply that coverage changes are not bound until formally processed and confirmed by the insurance carrier.
   - Required phrasing: *"Coverage changes or updates cannot be bound, altered, or bound via email without written confirmation from a licensed agent."*

2. **Client Responsibility Disclaimer**:
   - Always include the explicit policyholder responsibility statement:
   - Required phrasing: *"Please remember that final coverage selections and decisions remain the responsibility of the policyholder."*

3. **Anti-Steering & Non-Judgmental Standards**:
   - Do not make declarative legal assertions regarding a client's risk exposure.
   - Avoid terms like "underinsured", "legally deficient", or "uninsured".
   - Use comparison terminology: *"comparing your existing policy limits against updated replacement cost calculations and available optional endorsements."*

4. **Recommendation Framework**:
   - Position every policy modification or limit increase as a *recommendation* subject to client approval.
   - Always include: *"We always request your explicit permission before making any changes to your policy."*
